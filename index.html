YOUR FIELD GUIDE

A lab without
the lab coat.

Every experiment responds to your choices. Start with one parameter and watch what happens.

  1. Pick a field.VLSI takes you inside CMOS circuits. Signals connects noise, motion, and conversion. Control explores poles, damping, and feedback.
  2. Open an experiment.Use its sliders, draggable points, or circuit blocks. Each lab has its own controls and explanations.
  3. Compare and explore.Switch labs from the viewer's title menu. “All experiments” brings you back to the collection. “Open full page” gives a lab the whole browser window.
  4. Keep a useful link.Use Share to copy a link to the current lab. It opens the same experiment for anyone with the link; slider settings are not included.

These are educational models. Read each lab's notes for its assumptions and limits. A mouse and a larger screen are helpful for the 3D labs.
