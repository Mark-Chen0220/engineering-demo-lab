The camera misses a step.

Between frames the runner and marked blade advance more than half a cycle. The shorter path between samples points backward, even though the actual motion stays forward.

fₛ > 2f   →   resolve this cycle

One full lap = one marked propeller revolution. The sampled view holds each captured pose; it does not interpolate. The chart shows the fundamental sin(2πft). Body motion contains harmonics, so resolving the entire runner needs faster sampling. Different blade colors distinguish a full turn; identical blades would introduce extra symmetry.
